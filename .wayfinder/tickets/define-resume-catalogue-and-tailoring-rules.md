# Define resume catalogue and tailoring rules

## Question

What resume variants are required for the initial system, how does it select one for a job, and what tailoring is allowed without inventing candidate facts?

## Known context

- Different roles may require different resumes, but the initial catalogue is undecided.
- Resume choice is an input to the broad-versus-targeted application decision.
- The system needs to record the selected resume and its reason in the Dashboard.
